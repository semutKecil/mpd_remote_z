MPD Remote is an open-source Android client for Music Player Daemon (MPD). Control your MPD server directly from your Android device over your local network.

Features:
- Connect to one or more MPD servers over the network
- Browse your music library by albums, artists, tags, and files
- View and manage playlists
- Search your music collection
- See what is currently playing with detailed track information
- Control playback, repeat, random, single, and consume modes
- Adjust volume from the app
- Continue playback with background media controls
- Save multiple server connections for easy switching

Getting started:
- Install MPD Remote on your Android device
- Make sure your MPD server is running and reachable on your network
- Open the app and enter your server address, port, and credentials if required

MPD Remote is free and open source. Contributions, bug reports, and feature suggestions are welcome.
