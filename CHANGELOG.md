## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-19

### Added

- Connect to one or more MPD servers over the network
- Browse the music library by albums, artists, tags, and files
- View and manage playlists
- Search the music collection
- View the currently playing track
- Control playback (play/pause, next/previous, seek)
- Control repeat, random, single, and consume modes
- Adjust volume from the app
- Continue playback with background media controls
- Save multiple server connections for easy switching
